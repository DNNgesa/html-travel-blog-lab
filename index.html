Fire Island National Seashore

Visiting Fire Island National Seashore is an unforgettable escape into nature. Nestled just off the coast of Long Island, this pristine park offers a unique blend of wild, windswept beaches, historical landmarks, and diverse ecosystems. Walking through the Sunken Forest and climbing the iconic Fire Island Lighthouse were absolute highlights, showcasing the raw, untouched beauty of this coastal sanctuary.
